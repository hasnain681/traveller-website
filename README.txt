TRAVELLERS WEBSITE

Files:
- index.html: page structure and content
- style.css: responsive styling and layout
- script.js: mobile menu, billing toggle, demo newsletter interaction

How to run:
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Keep index.html, style.css and script.js in the same folder.
3. Open index.html in a browser.

Notes:
- Google Fonts and remote Unsplash/Pravatar images need an internet connection.
- App store links, navigation placeholders, pricing and newsletter are demo content.
  Replace them with your real app links, business details and backend integration.
